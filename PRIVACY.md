# Privacy Policy for dy2

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the dy2 Discord bot ("dy2", "the bot", "we", or
"us") processes information when it is installed in or used within a Discord
server.

## Information we process

Depending on the features enabled by a server administrator, dy2 may process:

- Discord user, server, role, channel, message, and emoji IDs;
- usernames, display names, role membership, avatars, join/leave events, bans,
  kicks, boosts, and other member or server update events;
- message content, embeds, attachments, and invite links when required for
  prefix commands, Invite Guard, message edit logs, and message deletion logs;
- command input supplied directly by a user, including anonymous-message and
  text-to-speech input;
- configuration such as logging-channel IDs, reaction-role mappings, invite
  protection allowlists, and anonymous-channel selections; and
- historical reward and point data, including user IDs, names, balances and
  activity history, retained from an older feature.

## How we use information

We use this information only to provide requested bot features, including
moderation, server event logs, welcome and goodbye messages, member counts,
reaction roles, invite-link protection, reports, anonymous messages, music,
and text-to-speech.

dy2 does not use Discord data for advertising, sell personal data, build
cross-server user profiles, or make automated decisions with legal or similarly
significant effects.

## Voice privacy

dy2 plays music and text-to-speech audio in voice channels. It does not receive,
record, or transcribe users' voice conversations. Text-to-speech output may be
stored temporarily as an audio file while it is played and is deleted afterward.

## Stored data and retention

- Deleted-message records may include the server ID, channel ID and name, author
  ID and name, message content, attachment URLs, and deletion time. Records older
  than seven days are removed when the bot next stores or retrieves these logs,
  or processes a deletion request. If the bot is idle or offline, older records
  can remain in the local file until the next such operation.
- Server configuration and allowlists remain until an administrator changes the
  configuration, the bot operator removes it, or the server requests deletion.
- Historical reward and point files may contain user IDs, names, balances, and
  activity history. These files remain until the operator removes them or a
  verified deletion request is completed; the current bot startup skips the
  economy module.
- Moderation and event messages sent into Discord channels remain subject to the
  server's settings and Discord's own retention practices. The bot does not
  control copies already sent to Discord.
- Reports submitted through the bot are delivered to the configured support
  channel and remain there until removed by an authorized operator.

## Third-party processing

dy2 uses Discord to deliver its service. When a user explicitly invokes certain
features, limited input may also be sent to the relevant provider:

- text submitted to a text-to-speech command may be sent to Google Translate's
  text-to-speech service through the gTTS library;
- music search terms or URLs may be sent to services such as YouTube or Spotify
  to resolve playable media; and
- attachment and media URLs may be retrieved from their hosting provider.

Those services process information under their own privacy terms. We do not
sell information to these providers.

## Access and security

Stored bot data is available only to the bot operator and infrastructure needed
to run the service. Within Discord, sensitive log commands are restricted using
Discord permissions such as Manage Messages or Manage Server. No method of
storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit collection and
retention to what is needed for the bot's functionality.

## Your choices and deletion requests

Users may run `s deletedata` (aliases: `s xoadulieu` and `s privacydelete`) to
delete their own retained deleted-message records. This command does not delete
messages already sent to Discord or server-wide configuration.

For deletion of other bot-held data, a server owner or affected user may contact
us at **vnggodcreative@gmail.com**. Please include the relevant Discord user ID
and/or server ID. We may request reasonable verification before completing a
request.

Server administrators can stop future processing by disabling the relevant
feature or removing dy2 from the server.

## Children's privacy

dy2 is intended for people permitted to use Discord under Discord's Terms of
Service and applicable law. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone
who is not permitted to use Discord.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the bot's features or legal requirements change.
The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

## Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to **vnggodcreative@gmail.com**.
